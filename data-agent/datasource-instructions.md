Hub is the FinOps hub serving database. Use these functions: Costs_v1_2() for cost and usage (one row per resource, per day, per charge), Prices_v1_2() for price sheet data, Recommendations_v1_2() for reservation recommendations, and CommitmentDiscountUsage_v1_2() for reservation and savings plan utilization. Prices, recommendations, and commitment usage may be empty if no EA/MCA billing exports are configured.
